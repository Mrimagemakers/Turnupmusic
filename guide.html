1Quick start: your first 10 minutes

  1. Open Turn Up Music on your phone, tablet, computer or Meta Quest browser.
  2. Enter your date of birth and agree to the Community Guidelines. Adults (18+) get everything; under 18 gets music mode.
  3. Pick a station from the list and tap ▶. Tap ☆ to save it to your favorites.
  4. Make your look: tap 🧑 My avatar, choose one of 50 characters, an outfit, a hat and glasses, and your name.
  5. Tap “VR Lounge” at the top to step into the 3D world. On a phone, drag to look and tap the floor to walk.
  6. Save your progress with the 👤 button at the top: create an account with a name and passcode, and your profile, favorites and settings save by themselves and come back on any device. Write your passcode down, because it cannot be reset.
  7. Change worlds with the world buttons. Try the Reggae Dance Hall, Downtown Tokyo or the Space Station.
  8. Join the party with 👥 Join the party to meet the other people in that world. Wave, dance, cheer and chat.
  9. Turn on your mic 🎤 to talk. People near you hear you louder.
  10. Open Turn Up Social (speech-bubble button) to set up your page, follow people and post.
  11. Got a headset? Tap Enter VR, put it on, and use the menu on your left wrist.

2Getting started

  1. Age check. The first time you open the app it asks for your date of birth. Only “18 or older: yes or no” is saved, never your birthday.
  2. Community Guidelines. Read and agree to them (be kind, no hate or harassment, no sexual content, nothing involving minors, no scams).
  3. Name and avatar. If you skip this, you get a guest name and a random character. You can change them any time under 🧑 My avatar.
  4. Install it (optional). On a phone, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen”. There's also an Android app.

Under 18? You'll be in music mode: every station, podcast and world is still yours to enjoy, but parties, voice chat, the social feed, events and memberships are switched off. See Safety.

3Radio, podcasts & your music

Listen to the radio

  1. Scroll the station list or pick a genre (hip-hop, R&B, reggae, pop, metal, yacht rock and more).
  2. Tap a station to play it. ⏮ ⏭ skip between stations, and the slider sets the volume.
  3. Tap ☆ Save to keep a station in Favorites. Add your own with a stream link in My stations.

Podcasts

  1. Open the 🎙 Podcasts tab and search for a show, or pick a suggested one.
  2. Tap Follow to keep it, then tap an episode to play. It remembers where you stopped.

Your own music and videos

  1. Open 🎵 My media and add songs or videos from your device (they stay on that device).
  2. Play a song yourself, put a video on the big screen in the lounge, or load it into the DJ booth.

4Your avatar & look

  1. Tap 🧑 My avatar (in the lounge) to open your profile and look.
  2. Choose a character (50 to pick from), then your height and glow color.
  3. Add a hat, eyewear, a chain or pearls and headphones.
  4. Dress up with outfits: solid, stripes and ombré are free. Plus members get every style (kente, galaxy, sequins, camo, leopard and more) and can print their own fabric.
  5. Add a short bio, your favorite genre and your Instagram, Spotify or YouTube links. Tap Save.
Want the same look on your tablet and your Quest? Link your devices: see Turn Up Social → Use on another device.

  4. On a headset, tap Enter VR. For AR on a phone, tap AR to put a mini stage on your table.

Graphics quality: 🔋 Battery saver, ⚖️ Standard, 💎 Ultra

Tap the ✨ button (💎 when Ultra is on) in the controls on the right side of the lounge to pick a level:

  • 💎 Ultra:
    • extra resolution and smooth, non-jagged edges
    • live shadows from everyone around you
    • razor-sharp textures on floors and walls
    • cinematic color, glow and a soft vignette
    Ultra turns on by itself on strong computers. If your device starts to struggle, it steps back to Standard once and tells you.
  • ⚖️ Standard: the usual look, and the default on phones and tablets.
  • 🔋 Battery saver: lower resolution and no glow, for the smoothest play on older phones.

In a VR headset the picture stays tuned for smooth, comfortable motion. On a PC headset, Ultra also renders the VR view a little sharper.

Seeing screens and videos clearly

  1. Zoom to read signs, see faces or check out details. Looking around slows down while you're zoomed in, so it's easier to aim.
  2. Watch up close glides you right in front of a screen so the video or page fills your view, with the menus tucked away. Tap ✕ Back to the room (or press Esc) to go back where you were.
  3. On a web page or YouTube screen, tapping the screen takes you up close so you can use the page (its play buttons, links and so on) directly.
  4. People and avatars standing in front of a screen now show in front of it, so videos no longer cover their heads.

The first time you open the lounge on a phone, a computer or a headset, a short "How to move around" sheet pops up for that device. You can open it again any time with 🧭.

On phones and tablets, tap Hide controls to minimize the dashboard and see more of the world.

7Parties, voice & touch

  1. In the lounge, tap 👥 Join the party. The first time, you'll see a short safety note.
  2. Everyone else in the same world appears as their avatar with a name tag. Use Join with code for a private party with friends.
  3. Use 💃 Dance, 🙌 Cheer, 👋 Wave and the 🔥 ❤️ 😂 🎉 reactions, and type in the chat.
  4. Tap 🎤 to talk. Voices come from where people are standing, and a 🔊 shows who's talking.
  5. Tap a guest's name or avatar (or touch them in VR) to see their card: View page, Follow, Mute, Hide/Block or 🚩 Report.
  6. Leave any time with Leave the party.

8Live events, flyers & invitations

Go to an event

  1. Tap the calendar button (📅 Live events) to see what's on, including the weekly featured shows.
  2. Tap I'm going or Add to calendar. When it starts, a red LIVE NOW banner appears in the lounge; tap it to join.

Host an event (VIP and up)

  1. Open Live events → Host an event. Enter a name, date, time, length, world, music and a description.
  2. Tap Publish event. When it starts, you're the DJ: everyone who joins hears your station.

Make a flyer or invitation

  1. After publishing (or with 🎨 Make a flyer in the form, or 🎨 Flyer & invites on your event), the flyer maker opens.
  2. Choose Flyer or Invitation and a look: Neon Nights, Gold Gala, Retro Sunset or Bold Poster. Choose a size: Post, Story or Square.
  3. Edit the headline and details, add a photo, and change the color. For an invitation, add the person's name and a note.
  4. Tap Share… (phone), Download, Email, or the X, Facebook, WhatsApp or Text buttons. The QR code opens your event.

9Turn Up Social

  1. Tap the speech-bubble button. The 🏠 Feed shows posts from everyone, or from friends and people you follow.
  2. Post text, a photo, a link, or 🎵 the song that's on. Like ❤️, reply 💬 and share ↗. Use 🚩 to report a post.
  3. Open 👤 My page and tap Edit my page to add a mood, your city, what you're into, a cover color and your Top friends.
  4. Tap anyone's name to see their page: their wall, photos, friends and followers. Follow them, or post on their wall.
  5. When you follow each other, you're friends. See them all in 👥 Friends, and search everyone in 🔎 Find people.
  6. 🔔 Alerts tells you about new followers, likes, replies and wall posts.

Use on another device (same account on your tablet, phone and Quest)

  1. On the device you already use: My page → 📱 Use on another device → Make a code for my other device.
  2. On the new device: My page → Use on another device, type the 8-character code (like K7Q2-M9XD) and tap I have a code.
  3. That's it: same name, avatar, outfit, friends and membership on both. The code works for 10 minutes. Never give it to anyone else.

10DJ booth, screens & camera

  1. 🎧 DJ booth (VIP): load stations or your own songs onto two decks, use the crossfader and effects (echo, horn, siren, talk-over), then Go live to the party.
  2. 📺 Screens: play YouTube videos or your own videos on the big screens for everyone (VIP), or watch on your own.
  3. 📷 Camera: take photos and videos in any world, save them, or share one straight to your wall.

11Nightclub Mogul: work the club, build an empire

Tap 🍾 Mogul on the party bar (or Party → 🍾 Nightclub Mogul in VR). Pick a job, take its free training first, then work real shifts for cash. Spend the cash on clubs of your own. It's 18+ only, like the rest of the social side of the app.

  1. Pick a job. Every job has a 🎓 Training shift that walks you through it one step at a time, and pays $50 the first time you finish it.
  2. Work a shift. You get paid for good work. Mistakes are strikes (❌), and three strikes ends the shift early.
  3. Level up. Every shift earns experience, so later shifts are busier and pay more.
  4. Become the owner. Under 👑 Club Owner, buy venues from a basement bar up to a club in orbit. Then add upgrades, hire staff, and press 🌙 Next night to see what each club earns.

13Safety & community

Turn Up Music is for adults 18 and older. It has live voice chat, public posts and parties with people you don't know.

How age protection works

  1. Everyone enters a date of birth the first time they open the app. Only “18+: yes or no” is kept, never the birthday.
  2. Under 18 gets music mode: radio, podcasts, their own music and the worlds on their own. Parties, voice chat, chat, Turn Up Social, events, flyers and memberships are off.
  3. The answer is locked on that device, so it can't simply be retried.
  4. Adults only ever see and hear other adults in parties. If you think someone is under 18, report them with “This person may be under 18”.

Your safety tools

  1. Mute: tap a guest → Mute (or Mute for me in VR).
  2. Block: on their card or page, choose Hide/Block. You won't see, hear or get posts from them. Unblock any time in the 🛡 Safety center.
  3. Report: 🚩 on a post, on a guest's card or on their page. Pick a reason and send. The person isn't told who reported them.
  4. 🛡 Safety center (shield button): only hear voice from friends, blur photos from strangers, show only friends' wall posts, and manage your blocked list.
  5. Leave: you can leave a party, switch worlds or turn off your mic at any time.

Community Guidelines

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate speech, threats or bullying.
  • No sexual content, and nothing involving anyone under 18, ever.
  • No spam, scams, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Don't share your address, passwords, sign-in codes or money details. Turn Up Music staff will never ask for them.
  • Moderators can mute, remove and block people from parties, and hide posts.
If anyone is in danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number). To report a child being exploited, use the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

14Troubleshooting & FAQ

How does karaoke work?

Tap 🎤 in the lounge controls (phones: the 🎤 button on the bottom bar; VR: Party, then 🎤 Karaoke). Pick a song and sing along with the lyrics on the big screen. Join the party so friends can hear you, see the same lyrics and cheer with 👏🔥🎉. You can add your own music file and lyrics under Use my own song (only music you have the right to use). Your score comes from how much you sing plus the cheers.

How do I change the pictures on the big screen?

While music plays the screen shows beat-reactive visuals. Tap 🎛 in the lounge controls to pick Orbs, Meter, Silhouettes, Tunnel, Lasers, Waves, Starfield or Kaleidoscope, or leave it on 🎲 Surprise me. In VR open the menu, go to Party, and tap the 🎛 button.

How do I switch worlds on a phone, tablet or in VR?

Tap 🌍 (or the 🌍 World button at the bottom) for a big list of worlds. In VR, the menu's Worlds tab lists them all, and Party → 🌍 Next world hops to the next one.

I don't hear any music

Browsers need one tap before they can play sound. Tap anywhere, or the Tap to turn on sound button. Check your volume and that your phone isn't on silent.

The Quest browser closed or VR got jumpy

Close other browser tabs, then open Turn Up Music again. The app keeps only two worlds in memory on a headset and dims extra lights in VR, but heavy multitasking can still run out of memory. A world you haven't visited recently takes a few seconds to rebuild.

My friend looks different on my tablet than in VR

Make sure both devices are online so they download the same characters. To be the same person everywhere, link your devices with Use on another device.

I can't hear someone's voice

They may be far away (voices get quieter with distance), muted, or blocked. If you turned on “Only hear voice chat from friends” in the Safety center, you'll only hear friends and people you follow.

I can't join the party or open the feed

These are for adults 18+. Some school and work networks also block live connections. Try another Wi-Fi network or your phone's data.

I paid but my plan isn't on

It can take up to a day to confirm. Tap I've paid, check my membership. If it's still off, contact the host with your member ID (in the ★ VIP screen).

Is my birthday saved?

No. Only whether you're 18 or older is saved, on your device.

15For the owner & moderators

  1. Sign in as the owner: ★ VIP → Owner sign-in → paste your private owner code. Never share it or put it in a file.
  2. Set prices and payment links for Plus, VIP and Pro under Owner tools (Stripe payment links work best; the buyer's member ID is added to the link).
  3. Give a membership: paste the buyer's member ID, pick Plus, VIP, Pro or VIP + Moderator and a length, then tap Give membership.
  4. Moderators see a Reports list in the 🛡 Safety center, can hide posts, and can mute or remove guests from any party. Pro members can moderate their own event parties.
  5. Underage reports: remove the person from the party and, if needed, hide their posts. Age checks rely on what people enter, so reports from the community matter.
